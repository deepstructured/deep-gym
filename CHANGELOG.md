# Changelog

## 1.7.0

- Add your gyms in Settings and set up each gym’s bar and plates. New workouts remember the last gym you selected. You can also log a workout without a gym.
- Existing exercises are available in every gym, including gyms you add later. When creating or editing an exercise, you can limit it to one or more gyms and keep its training history.
- A blank workout starts on the current local date, even if the app restores an old empty draft. A workout in progress keeps its date across midnight, and you can still choose a date for a past session.
- Weights and reps use more legible numerals. Switching between screens no longer flashes the startup loader.

## 1.6.1

- Improved sign-in recovery, session handling and account switching.
- Added retry states for failed data loads and reduced repeated requests between screens.
- Fixed workout save retries, draft synchronization and incomplete history reads.
- Updated dependencies and strengthened checks against publishing private files or credentials.
